FAQ For Promotional Products

  • Ordering

  • Do you support bulk branding orders for corporate events?
    Yes. We handle bulk promotional product orders for corporate events, launches, employee kits and client gifting. Share quantity, delivery date, logo files, item preference and packaging requirements so we can plan sourcing, branding, proofing and dispatch together.

  • Materials

  • What promotional materials do you offer?
    Common promotional materials include textiles, drinkware, stationery, office items, tech accessories, trophies, bags, eco products and custom packaging. The best option depends on audience, budget, campaign purpose, branding area and whether the item must be portable for events.

  • Delivery

  • Can you deliver promotional products to multiple locations?
    Yes. We can coordinate delivery to event venues, company branches, client offices and staff locations across the UAE. For multi-location drops, send the quantity per address, preferred delivery date and packaging instructions before production begins.
