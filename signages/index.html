  • Material

  • What is the best material for outdoor signages in UAE?
    Outdoor signage in the UAE commonly uses ACP (Aluminum Composite Panels), acrylic, coated metal, aluminum, high-grade vinyl, and LED-ready sign boxes. The best choice depends on sunlight exposure, mounting surface, viewing distance, and whether the sign needs long-term exterior durability against heat and dust.
  • What is the difference between traditional neon and LED-flex signs?
    Traditional neon uses glass tubes filled with gas, which are fragile and high-voltage. LED-flex (or "Neon Flex") uses flexible silicone or PVC housing with LED strips inside. LED-flex is more energy-efficient, durable, safer for interiors, and easier to shape into complex brand logos.

  • Lighting

  • Do you provide illuminated sign board options?
    Yes. We produce illuminated sign boards for storefronts, mall units, reception areas, and road-facing locations. Options include front-lit or back-lit acrylic faces, 3D channel letters, LED modules, and neon-style LED flex for day-and-night visibility.

  • Wayfinding

  • Can you handle complete wayfinding signage projects?
    Yes. We manage end-to-end wayfinding projects including site surveys, sign schedule planning, directional logic, room IDs, and installation. Our systems focus on clear visitor movement and consistent branding across large facilities.

  • Permits & Installation

  • Do I need a permit for outdoor signage in Dubai?
    Most outdoor permanent signage in Dubai requires approval from authorities like Dubai Municipality, RTA, or specific developers (e.g., Emaar, Nakheel). We can provide technical drawings and specifications required for your permit application process.
  • How long does it take to manufacture and install a sign board?
    Standard sign production typically takes 5–10 working days depending on complexity (e.g., 3D letters vs. flat panels). Installation timelines vary by location; mall or high-rise installations often require specific timings or equipment like cranes or scaffolding.

  • Maintenance

  • How do I maintain my outdoor sign board in the UAE weather?
    Due to dust and high temperatures in the UAE, we recommend periodic cleaning with soft water and non-abrasive cloths. For illuminated signs, ensure the power supply (transformers/drivers) is kept in a ventilated, dust-free area if possible to prolong LED life.
