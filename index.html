  • UV Printing Services

  • What materials can be used for UV printing in the UAE?
    Our advanced UV printing technology allows us to print directly onto a wide variety of rigid and flexible substrates. Popular materials include acrylic, glass, metal (aluminium, steel), wood, MDF, PVC, foam board, ACP (Aluminium Composite Panel), and vinyl sticker media.
  • Is UV printing suitable for outdoor signage in Dubai's weather?
    Yes, UV printing is highly durable and water-resistant, making it excellent for outdoor use in the UAE. The UV curing process makes the ink resistant to fading from sunlight. For extreme conditions or high-touch areas, we can apply additional lamination or protective coatings to extend the lifespan of the print.
  • What is the difference between UV printing and standard sticker mounting?
    Direct UV printing applies ink directly onto the rigid surface (like acrylic or metal), resulting in a cleaner, more premium finish without the risk of edges peeling or lifting over time. Sticker mounting involves printing on vinyl and applying it to a board, which is cost-effective but may not offer the same high-end look as direct UV printing.

  • Large Format Printing

  • What types of large format printing do you provide for events and exhibitions?
    We offer complete large format solutions ideal for events, exhibitions, and retail launches. This includes custom wallpaper prints, vinyl stickers, high-contrast posters, fabric prints (soft signage), flags, stage backdrops, and portable rollup and popup stand systems.
  • Can large format prints withstand outdoor conditions?
    Yes. When we produce large format prints for outdoor use, such as construction site hoardings or outdoor banners, we select weather-resistant media and apply appropriate finishing techniques to ensure they withstand heat, sunlight, and moisture.
  • What file specifications are required for large format printing?
    For the best results at scale, we require high-resolution, print-ready files (PDF, AI, EPS, or high-res PSD). Files should be in CMYK color mode, with fonts outlined, and include the correct dimensions and bleed areas to ensure crisp, accurate output.

  • Signages & Wayfinding

  • What types of custom signages do you manufacture?
    We design, produce, and install a wide variety of signages, including indoor reception and departmental signs, durable outdoor exterior signs, safety and compliance signages, comprehensive wayfinding systems, illuminated sign boards, and custom neon/LED-flex signs.
  • Do you provide installation services for signages in Dubai?
    Yes, we offer professional installation support for all our signage projects. Our team ensures that your indoor, outdoor, or wayfinding signages are securely and correctly installed at your location, whether it's a retail store, corporate office, or event venue.
  • What is the best material for outdoor sign boards in the UAE?
    The best material depends on the specific location and viewing distance, but we commonly recommend Aluminium Composite Panels (ACP), coated metals, and high-grade acrylics for outdoor signages in the UAE. These materials offer excellent resistance to heat and UV exposure, ensuring long-lasting brand visibility.
